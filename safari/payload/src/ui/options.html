valyou

Filters racist, hateful, sexist, violent and rage-baiting content out of your Facebook and Instagram feed, ads, and messages — entirely on your device.

What to filter

Each category has an action and a sensitivity. Lower sensitivity filters more and produces more false positives; the default values are tuned to err toward leaving things visible.

Where to filter

Videos

valyou reads a post's text — caption, title, labels — but cannot watch the video itself, so a hateful video under a clean caption can slip past text filtering. Control what videos are allowed to do.

Smart detection on-device

A small machine-learning model, bundled with the extension, catches hateful phrasing the pattern rules miss. It runs entirely on your device — no network, no account — and is capped so it can only reinforce the pattern rules, never hide anything on its own. Turn it off to rely on patterns alone.

Try it

Paste any text to see exactly how the on-device classifier scores it. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Your rules

Security & privacy

valyou works entirely on your device. It makes no network requests, sends nothing anywhere, and needs no account or API. Everything it stores — your settings and its counters — is encrypted with AES-256-GCM before it touches disk.

Your data

valyou stores counters, not content. No post text, author name, or URL is ever written to disk or sent anywhere — there is nowhere for it to go.
