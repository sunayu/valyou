# valyou — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-08-05_

valyou is a content-filtering browser. It hides hateful, violent, and
rage-baiting content on the social networks you choose to browse. This policy
describes exactly what valyou does and does not do with your information.

## The short version

**valyou collects no personal data, sends nothing to us or to any third party,
and has no servers.** All filtering happens on your device. We cannot see what
you browse, what gets filtered, or anything about you — because none of it ever
leaves your phone.

## What runs on your device (and stays there)

- **The content filter.** The classifier, word lists, and the machine-learning
  model are bundled inside the app. Text on the pages you view is analyzed
  locally, in memory, and discarded. It is never uploaded.
- **Your settings.** Your filtering preferences (which categories to hide, video
  policy, etc.) are stored in your device's secure keystore (Apple Keychain /
  Android Keystore). They are encrypted at rest, tied to your device, and never
  synced to the cloud or to us.
- **Anonymous counters.** The app keeps simple local tallies — e.g. "posts
  filtered" — so it can show you how much it's doing. These are numbers only,
  contain no content, author, or URL, are stored on-device, and are never
  transmitted.

## What we do not do

- We do **not** collect, store, or transmit your name, email, contacts,
  location, browsing history, or the content of any page.
- We do **not** use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
- We do **not** track you across apps or websites.
- We do **not** have accounts, logins, or servers of our own.

## The websites you browse

valyou shows you third-party websites (such as Facebook, Instagram, and X) inside
an in-app browser. When you sign in to and use those sites, **your relationship
is with them, and their own privacy policies and terms apply** to that activity,
exactly as if you had opened them in Safari or Chrome. valyou does not read,
store, or relay your credentials or your session; login happens directly between
your device and the site. valyou only ever _hides_ content those sites send to
your device — it never alters, reposts, or exfiltrates anything.

## Children

valyou does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change and the new
version will ship with the app.
