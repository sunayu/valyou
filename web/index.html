How it works

A classifier that lives on your device.

Every post is read, scored, and acted on in the moment — locally, in memory, and then discarded. No cloud call, no external AI service, nothing to subscribe your data to.

What it catches

Five things you didn't ask to see.

Turn each category on or off, and decide what happens when it's found. These are the defaults.

Racial hate

Hide

Slurs, dehumanizing language, and coded racist tropes — including obfuscated and “dog-whistle” variants.

Gendered & sexist hate

Hide

Misogynistic and gender-based attacks, harassment framed as “just an opinion.”

Violence & threats

Hide

Calls for harm, glorified violence, and language that targets people or groups.

Harassment

Blur

Pile-ons, demeaning insults, and content aimed at wearing a person down.

Rage bait

Posts engineered purely to provoke — the outrage machine's raw material.

Private by design

Nothing you browse ever leaves your device.

valyou has no servers and no accounts. The classifier, the model, and the encryption all live on your phone or in your browser. It can't see your feed — because your feed never reaches it.

That's also why it works without any paid AI service behind it: the intelligence is bundled in, not rented.
